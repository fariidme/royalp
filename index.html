About Royal Elevators

A trusted partner in vertical mobility

Royal Elevators & Escalators Ltd. is a Mogadishu-based provider of elevator and escalator solutions for residential, commercial and public buildings across Somalia.

We bring professional installation, dependable maintenance and modern upgrade expertise together under one roof — helping building owners and developers move people safely, smoothly and efficiently.

Our Process

From first conversation to ongoing care

A clear, structured process designed around your project — adapted to the specific requirements of every building.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We listen to your needs, building type and goals to understand the right solution for your project.

  2. 02

    Site Assessment

    A detailed evaluation of your site, shaft and space to inform the design and installation approach.

  3. 03

    Solution Design

    We propose the right equipment, configuration and finishes tailored to your building and budget.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Professional installation by experienced technicians, carried out safely and coordinated around your schedule.

  5. 05

    Testing & Handover

    Rigorous testing and commissioning, followed by a full handover of a safe, fully operational system.

  6. 06

    Ongoing Maintenance

    Scheduled servicing and responsive support that keep your equipment running reliably for the long term.
